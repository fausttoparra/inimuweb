3.0FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Anything else, ask us on Discord. A human answers, usually within minutes.

Which games are supported?

Counter-Strike 2, Rust, Apex Legends, Escape from Tarkov, PUBG: Battlegrounds and Rainbow Six Siege. Check the status board for the current state of each.

What happens when a game updates?

The title switches to “Updating” and the loader won’t start it until a verified build ships. Any time you lose is added back to your key.

Does it work with streaming and recording?

Yes. The overlay is only drawn for your display, so captures from OBS, Discord or in-game replays show the game as normal.

How fast is delivery?

Instant. Your key appears in your dashboard and inbox as soon as the payment clears.

Can I move my key to a new PC?

Keys are bound to one machine. You can reset the binding yourself from the dashboard once every seven days.

Is there a refund policy?

All sales are final, since access is delivered instantly. If we fail to deliver what you bought, open a ticket on Discord and we'll make it right. Full details are in the terms.
