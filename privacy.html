Privacy notice

What we store, why we store it, and how to have it removed.

01In short

  • We never ask for your email address or your real name.
  • We don't sell, rent or hand your data to anyone.
  • We keep only what the account needs to work, and we erase it when you ask.

02What we keep

When you create an account and use inimu, the following is stored on infrastructure we run ourselves:

  • Your username and a one-way hash of your password (bcrypt). We can't read your password.
  • The UID given to your account, if one has been assigned.
  • Basic account timestamps: created, last sign-in and last change.
  • Your Discord ID, only if you decide to link it.
  • Your current and past licenses: game, expiry date and number of resets.
  • A hardware fingerprint (HWID) while a license is active on a machine.

Payments go through our payment provider. Card details never reach us and are never stored by us.

03Why we keep it

Nothing on that list is extra. The username and password hash let you sign in. The UID and license records are what you paid for. The HWID fingerprint is how we keep each license on one machine and stop keys from being shared.

04Server logs

For security and abuse prevention our servers keep brief access logs: IP address, the route requested, the time and the response code. They are rotated automatically and deleted after 14 days at most.

05Cookies

There is one cookie: a first-party, secure, HttpOnly session cookie that keeps you signed in. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising cookies and no cross-site identifiers.

06Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and everything tied to it at any time through Discord. Deletion happens right away and cannot be undone: your username becomes available again and any UID you held goes back into circulation.

You can also ask for a copy of what we hold about you, which is the short list in section 02, through the same channel.

07Contact

For anything about privacy, open a ticket on our Discord server. We use Discord instead of email on purpose: we don't collect email addresses, and it lets us confirm the request really comes from the account owner.
